Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 3, 2026

JOTMA is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account details such as name, email, phone number, and city.
  • Booking details including service, barber, date, time, and notes.
  • Device/browser data for security, analytics, and troubleshooting.

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage accounts and appointments.
  • To send booking confirmations, reminders, and service notifications.
  • To improve site performance, reliability, and fraud protection.

Data Sharing

We share booking information with the selected barber to fulfill your appointment. We may also use trusted service providers for messaging and analytics.

Retention

We keep booking and account records as needed for service operations, legal obligations, and dispute resolution.

Your Choices

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting support.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support.
